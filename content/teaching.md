        I teach computational methods to physics and astronomy students, from the
        bachelor to the Ph.D. level. The courses share a practical through-line:
        students work at a terminal, write and debug their own Python, and run the
        kind of numerical and Bayesian analyses my research relies on. Course
        materials are published openly wherever the format allows.

        ### Metodi computazionali di base per la fisica
        Università di Cagliari · 24 hours, 3 credits · 2025–present
        Seminar course on basic computational methods for physics. Taught at M.Sc.
        level, though bachelor and Ph.D. students also attend.

        ### Python Programming in Astrophysics & Cosmology
        SISSA, Ph.D. programme · 8 hours, 1 credit · 2023–present
        Mandatory second-term course for first-year Astrophysics & Cosmology Ph.D.
        students. Scientific Python for research work.
        *Materials:* [py_programming_APC](https://github.com/TommasoRonconi/py_programming_APC)

        ### Crash Python Course
        SISSA, Ph.D. programme · 10 hours, 1.5 credits · 2023–present
        Mandatory first-term course for first-year Astrophysics & Cosmology Ph.D.
        students. A fast introduction to Python for incoming graduate students.
        *Materials:* [crash_python_course](https://github.com/TommasoRonconi/crash_python_course)

        ### Hitchhiker's Guide to the Terminal
        SISSA, Ph.D. programme · 6 hours · 2021–2022
        A primer to \*nix filesystems, collaborative development and parallel
        computing — the tooling that scientific software assumes you already know.
        *Materials:* [hitchhikers_guide](https://github.com/TommasoRonconi/hitchhikers_guide)

        ### Enrico Veraldi — Ph.D., SISSA · 2025–present
        Supervisor. The impact of emission lines on the spectral energy distribution of
        star-forming galaxies, extending [GalaPy](/software/galapy/) to panchromatic
        line emission.

        ### Martina Torsello — M.Sc., Università di Trieste · 2021–2022
        Supervisor. *Numerical and scientific validation of GalaPy, the galactic
        spectral modelling tool for Python.* Co-author on the
        [library paper](/publications/2024-aa-685-a-161-r/).

        ### Sofia Contarini — M.Sc., Università di Bologna · 2018
        Co-supervisor. *Cosmological exploitation of the size function of cosmic voids
        identified in the distribution of biased tracers.* The work led to
        [Contarini, Ronconi et al. 2019](/publications/2019-mnras-488-3526-c/), later
        adopted in Euclid preparatory forecasts.
